# Community Code of Conduct

SMMly aims to maintain a respectful, constructive environment for everyone participating in its GitHub projects.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give clear, actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Insults, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, malicious links, or knowingly deceptive contributions.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the contact channel published at https://smmly.co. Do not use public issues for private or sensitive reports.
