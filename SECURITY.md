# Security Policy

SMMly takes the security of its platform, users, and integrations seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.

Use the contact channel published on the official website:

https://smmly.co

Include a clear description of the affected area, potential impact, and steps needed to reproduce the issue. Do not include real customer information, credentials, API keys, or other secrets.

## What to expect

- Reports will be reviewed when received.
- We may request additional information to understand and validate a report.
- Please allow a reasonable period for investigation and remediation before making details public.

## Scope

This policy applies to SMMly-owned services and repositories. Do not access, modify, or disrupt data or systems that you do not own or have explicit permission to test.
